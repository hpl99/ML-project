      Model Card: Known Weaknesses & Constraints

      • Conservative slope lower bound: 90m Copernicus DEM smooths micro-cliffs and roadside road-cuts.
      • Atmospheric reanalysis resolution: 25km ERA5 grid can overlook localized convective cloudbursts in deep ravines.
      • Pseudo-absence assumptions: Negative locations represent unrecorded landslide spots on land rather than proven permanent geotechnical stability.
      • Reporting bias: Global inventory catalogues are heavily weighted toward transit highways and populated communities.

      Method and safety

      Engineering architecture, data provenance, and public safety commitment.

      Relative Hazard Scoring Methodology

      URsafe formulates slope hazard as a Relative Risk Score (0 to 100) rather than an absolute event probability. Because historical landslide inventories contain reporting biases and negatives are sampled pseudo-absences on land, the output indicates localized comparative vulnerability relative to regional norms.

      Real Scientific Data Sources

      URsafe operates entirely on open, authoritative scientific data without synthetic fillers:

      • NASA COOLR / Global Landslide Catalog: Real historical landslide events verified from 2007 onward.
      • Copernicus DEM (GLO-90): 90-meter digital elevation model via Open-Meteo for Horn's slope, aspect, and curvature.
      • ECMWF ERA5 & Forecast: Daily reanalysis and forecast precipitation series for multi-day accumulation windows.
      • NASA POWER: Surface and root-zone soil wetness parameters.
      • Natural Earth Physical: High-resolution 1:10m river and lake centerlines.
